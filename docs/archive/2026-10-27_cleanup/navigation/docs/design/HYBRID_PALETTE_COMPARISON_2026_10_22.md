## Notion reference analysis and application

Source: [Notion homepage](https://www.notion.com/), retrieved 2026-10-22 with Firecrawl branding/component extraction, plus the owner's four homepage screenshots. The screenshots show a white/grey foundation and dark ordinary text; saturated blue concentrates on calls to action, while the secondary action uses a pale blue fill. Extracted homepage component values: primary button `#0075DE` / white text; secondary `#E6F3FE` / `#005BAB`; background white and ordinary primary text `#1A1A1A`. These are retrieval observations of the marketing site, not an official Notion product token specification. The supplied task-board image shows softly tinted compact status tags (purple, amber, blue, green) on very pale columns. Category icons and chart series have separate colours. Large ordinary reading surfaces stay neutral. Screenshot colours for those tags/charts were interpreted by role, not claimed as exact HEX.

For Culmina, the transferred principle is selective semantic colour. Existing owner direction keeps ordinary controls neutral; marketing blue CTAs do not override that decision. Saved is a stable green tag, Tight an amber tag, and Save failed a red tag. Their text/icons preserve non-colour meaning. Palette families identify weather quantities; they do not redefine success/failure. Dark-mode tag fills and contrast-adapted data tones are Culmina proposals, since the provided Notion screenshots show light mode only.

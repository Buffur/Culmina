   1  # Culmina — shared agent instructions
   2  
   3  Culmina is a Flutter/Dart Android astrophotography planner and observing log.
   4  The technical Dart package is `astroplan`; keep existing imports and identifiers.
   5  Plan a night, site, target and rig; save a Capture Plan; record imaging results.
   6  The app does not control cameras or mounts.
   7

  17  ## Architecture map
  18  
  19  - `lib/main.dart`: startup, catalog seeding and concrete dependency composition.
  20  - `lib/presentation/app_view_models.dart`: injected interfaces, services and ViewModels.
  21  - `lib/presentation/`: screens, shared UI, widgets and screen-scoped Provider/ViewModels.
  22  - `lib/presentation/navigation/app_router.dart`: go_router shell and root overlay routes.
  23  - Bottom tabs: Tonight, Logbook, Library and Settings; Plan opens above the shell.
  24  - Library contains rigs, targets and sites; Night, Moon and Weather have detail views.
  25  - `lib/domain/`: models, repository interfaces and astronomical/capture calculations.
  26  - `lib/data/`: Drift repositories, preferences, HTTP, backup and platform adapters.
  27  - `lib/core/`: clocks/time, units, diagnostics, configuration and theme tokens.
  28  - `lib/data/database/app_database.dart`: native SQLite database, currently schema v27.
  29  - `lib/data/database/schema_versions.dart` and `lib/data/database/generated_migrations/`: migrations.
  30  - `drift_schemas/`: exported JSON snapshots; `build.yaml` configures Drift generation.
  31  - `assets/catalog/catalog_v2.json`: bundled OpenNGC subset; filename is not catalog version.
  32  - `test/`: core/data/domain/presentation regressions; `test/support/` contains shared fakes.
  33  - `integration_test/` and `test_driver/`: host flows and separate Android capture routes.
  34  - `tool/`: verification, CI partitioning, catalog generation and design helper scripts.
  35  - `design/`: currently empty; historical browser helpers require missing lab inputs.
  36  - `.audit/atlas/index.html`: Visual Atlas; manifest, boards and captures accompany it.
  37  - `.audit/` and `docs/archive/`: historical evidence; inspect as needed, preserve contents.
  38  - `.agents/skills/`: active project recipes; `.agents/rules/` holds compatibility pointers.

  40  ## Technical invariants
  41  
  42  - Keep UI, ViewModels, domain interfaces/calculations and data implementations separated.
  43  - Reuse Provider, repository injection and existing router/draft guards for focused changes.
  44  - Keep catalogs, saved plans, calculations and Logbook useful offline.
  45  - Forecast retrieval, map tiles and optional place-name lookup need network access.
  46  - Preserve weather cache/freshness/error states and opt-in reverse-geocoding consent.
  47  - Unknown is not zero: preserve missing values, units, provenance and partial observations.
  48  - Never invent sensor specifications, catalog values, measurements or a quality score.
  49  - Relative stacking gain is not absolute SNR; storage estimates retain their evidence kind.
  50  - AF/MF and white balance are descriptive; do not invent numeric focus measurements.
  51  - SessionNight is 24 hours from site mean solar noon in UTC; retain zone/DST display semantics.
  52  - A placeholder location is not a selected site; preserve no-site and missing-input states.
  53  - CaptureBudgetCalculator and FitAnalyzer share atomic events; keep timing assumptions aligned.
  54  - Preserve draft, explicit Save plan, fit verdict and recorded-result distinctions.
  55  - Preserve local records, supported migrations and backup/restore compatibility.
  56  - Maintain Light, Dark and Field Red, including the root field color filter.
  57  - Theme definitions live in `lib/core/theme/`; inspect semantic roles and actual callers.
  58  - Use existing Geist typography, spacing, radii and reduced-motion handling for local UI fixes.
  59  - Keep meaningful words, accessible actions, text scaling and reachable controls.

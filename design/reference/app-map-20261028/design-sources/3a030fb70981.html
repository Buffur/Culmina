   1  # Architecture and data
   2  
   3  This describes the current `lib/` implementation and `pubspec.yaml`.
   4  
   5  ## Modules
   6  
   7  - `lib/main.dart`: initializes licenses, staged restore, database and catalog
   8    seeding; constructs concrete repositories/services and the widget root.
   9  - `lib/presentation/`: screens, shared UI, widgets, navigation and ViewModels.
  10  - `lib/domain/models/`: targets, rigs, sites, nights, plans, weather and results.
  11  - `lib/domain/repositories/`: storage and preferences interfaces.
  12  - `lib/domain/services/`: astronomy, visibility, Moon, candidates, imaging
  13    opportunities, budgets, fit, calibration, storage and session logic.
  14  - `lib/domain/metadata/`: bounded file readers and metadata result types.
  15  - `lib/domain/equipment_import/`: equipment matching and specification estimates.
  16  - `lib/data/`: Drift persistence, SharedPreferences repositories, network/device
  17    adapters, metadata file access, export and backup implementations.
  18  - `lib/core/`: math, clock, units, configuration, diagnostics and theme tokens.
  19  
  20  UI calls ViewModels; ViewModels use domain interfaces and services.
  21  Data adapters implement those interfaces. Astronomy/math calculations are
  22  separate from Flutter presentation.
  23  
  24  ## State and navigation
  25  
  26  `AppViewModels` constructs dependencies in `presentation/app_view_models.dart`.
  27  Provider exposes shared ViewModel instances, predominantly through
  28  `ChangeNotifierProvider`; lifecycle and candidate coordinators use `Provider`.
  29  Widgets consume state through Provider reads and watches.
  30  
  31  - `SiteViewModel`: active site, position and local-time context.
  32  - `SessionPlanViewModel`: editable target, rig, night and capture blocks.
  33  - `PlanLifecycleViewModel` / `CurrentSession`: restore, open, new, copy,
  34    save and discard of the current plan.
  35  - `NightConditionsViewModel`, `CandidatesViewModel`,
  36    `CaptureAnalysisViewModel`: forecast/astronomy, candidates and plan analysis.
  37  - `StartupViewModel` / `TonightViewModel`: loading and first-run/home state.
  38  - `GearViewModel`, `TargetsViewModel`, `SessionsViewModel`: library/logbook state.
  39  - `ResultsViewModel`, `MetadataImportViewModel`, `BackupViewModel` and
  40    `AstroBinExportViewModel`: their respective workflows.
  41  - Settings, display, theme and disclosure ViewModels persist user choices.
  42  
  43  `go_router` defines a stateful indexed shell for Tonight, Logbook, Library
  44  and Settings. Plan, results, choosers and Night/Moon/Weather open above the tabs.
  45  Router restoration returns to the appropriate list when object arguments
  46  cannot be restored. The plan flow prompts for unsaved changes.
  47  
  48  ## Domain data
  49  
  50  | Model | Contents |
  51  | --- | --- |
  52  | `AstroTarget` / `TargetAlias` | Catalog/custom identity, J2000 RA/Dec in degrees, type, optional size/magnitude, source and searchable aliases. |
  53  | `EquipmentProfile` | Flattened rig/camera view: sensor dimensions, resolution, pixel pitch, focal length/ratio, camera class, tracking, exposure limit and provenance. |
  54  | `LocationProfile` | Coordinates, optional elevation, IANA zone, manual Bortle/SQM context, sources/dates and notes. |
  55  | `CalendarDate` / `SessionNight` | Evening date and a half-open 24-hour UTC window beginning at site mean solar noon. |
  56  | `CaptureBlock` | Frame type, exposure seconds, count, filter, binning, typed ISO/gain, calibration policy, white balance, AF/MF, origins and file evidence. |
  57  | `SessionPlan` / `PlanOverrides` | Site/target/rig references, night, ordered blocks, labels, tracking override and optional per-plan budget/storage values. |
  58  | `Session` / `SessionLog` | Lifecycle, references, timestamps, snapshots, blocks, counts, results and notes; legacy records remain readable. |
  59  | `SessionSnapshot` | Versioned JSON of saved context, preferences, budget, opportunity and weather. |
  60  | `ResultReport` | Completed as planned, partial per-block counts, or not done with optional reason and observed conditions. |
  61  | `WeatherSnapshot` / `NightWeather` | Provider/model, fetch time, coordinates, hourly values, freshness, cache/failure/out-of-range state. |
  62  
  63  `SessionStatus` includes `draft`, `planned`, `inProgress`, `completed` and
  64  `abandoned`. Repository methods enforce lifecycle transitions. Execution
  65  models, events and `ExecutionMachine` remain in the code; the router has no
  66  dedicated live-tracker screen. Results are user reports of acquisition.
  67  
  68  Night identity uses the site's evening date. Time contexts display an IANA
  69  zone or mean solar time; DST changes local labels, not the UTC night window.
  70  Unknown optional values are null. Units and provenance are explicit;
  71  ISO/gain does not model photon collection.
  72  
  73  ## Persistence
  74  
  75  `data/database/app_database.dart` defines Drift schema **27** in
  76  `astroplan.sqlite` under the application documents directory.
  77  The supported migration floor is version 8; unsupported older/newer databases
  78  take the recovery path rather than being silently reinterpreted.
  79  
  80  | Tables | Stored data |
  81  | --- | --- |
  82  | `Devices` → `CameraModules` → `OpticalRigs` | Device/camera/optics chain, specifications, provenance and capture-format evidence. |
  83  | `LocationProfiles` | Saved sites and optional sky-quality context. |
  84  | `AstroTargets`, `TargetAliases` | Targets and catalog-designation/name aliases. |
  85  | `SessionLogs` | Session aggregate, status, references, timestamps, snapshots, overrides, result kind and notes. |
  86  | `CaptureBlocks` | Ordered blocks, settings, calibration policy, evidence and completed/rejected counters. |
  87  | `SessionEvents` | Ordered event history used to reconstruct execution state. |
  88  
  89  SQLite foreign keys are enabled; blocks/events cascade with session deletion,
  90  while camera/device references restrict deletion.
  91  SharedPreferences stores planning/display/privacy/first-run preferences,
  92  planner selection state and weather snapshots.
  93  `FileBackupService` and `BackupStaging` validate and stage local restore;
  94  startup applies it before opening the live database.
  95  
  96  ## External adapters and dependencies
  97  
  98  - Open-Meteo weather through `http`, with cached snapshots.
  99  - GPS through `geolocator`; device zone through `flutter_timezone`/`timezone`.
 100  - OpenStreetMap tiles through `flutter_map`/`latlong2`; opt-in Nominatim lookup.
 101  - Android file picker/range reads through a custom metadata platform channel.
 102  - DNG, JPEG, HEIF still-image and FITS metadata readers in the domain.
 103  - Backup uses `file_picker` and `archive`; export uses `share_plus`;
 104    external links use `url_launcher`.
 105  - Drift code generation uses `drift_dev` and `build_runner`.

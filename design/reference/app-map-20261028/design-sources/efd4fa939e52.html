   1  # Product specification
   2  
   3  Culmina plans astrophotography sessions and records their outcomes.
   4  This document describes implemented behavior in `lib/`, not a release plan.
   5  
   6  ## Main flow
   7  
   8  Choose site and evening date → choose target and rig → inspect the night →
   9  edit capture blocks → review budget/fit → save → image outside the app →
  10  record a result and review it in Logbook.
  11  
  12  ## Screens
  13  
  14  | Screen | Current behavior |
  15  | --- | --- |
  16  | Welcome | First-run setup and entry into planning. |
  17  | Tonight | Site/night context, Night/Moon/Weather summaries, target selection/candidates, planner access and results due for recording. |
  18  | Plan | Site/night, target and rig selection, capture blocks, opportunity/fit analysis, save, new plan and copy to another night. |
  19  | Target candidates | Evaluates catalog targets against the selected night's conditions and preferences. |
  20  | Night / Moon | Night timeline, darkness and altitude context, Moon information and calendar/night browsing. |
  21  | Weather | Hourly forecast in compact rows or charts, details, local time, provenance, freshness and unavailable/partial data states. |
  22  | Logbook | Saved sessions/results, filters, entry details, result recording, sharing/export and target progress. |
  23  | Progress | Integration and session totals by target/filter from completed non-legacy sessions with recorded block counts. |
  24  | Library | Manage rigs, targets and sites; detail/edit screens and scoped selection flows. |
  25  | Metadata import | Android file selection, metadata review and equipment matching/confirmation before saving a rig. |
  26  | Settings | Appearance, units, planning defaults, privacy, backup/data and About/licenses. |
  27  | Database recovery | Reports unsupported database versions and offers an explicit reset flow. |
  28  
  29  Bottom navigation contains Tonight, Logbook, Library and Settings.
  30  Plan and detail/selection flows open separately above those tabs.
  31  
  32  ## Capture planning
  33  
  34  - Ordered light, dark, flat, bias and dark-flat blocks with exposure/count,
  35    filter and contextual ISO/gain/binning settings.
  36  - Calibration timing policies: in-window, outside-window or reused library.
  37    Helpers expose inherited settings and mismatch warnings.
  38  - Budget includes integration, frame overheads and applicable optional
  39    setup, dither, refocus, filter-change and meridian-flip times.
  40  - Camera class, tracking choice/override and applicable in-camera noise
  41    reduction affect the relevant planning calculations.
  42  - Visibility/darkness/Moon and optional cloud gates determine usable windows.
  43    Fit compares plan load with opportunity and configured margins.
  44  - Storage distinguishes file evidence, declared/measured sizes and theoretical
  45    estimates. Relative stacking gain does not claim absolute physical SNR.
  46  - White balance, AF/MF and field provenance can be recorded.
  47    ISO/gain and AF/MF remain descriptive.
  48  - Draft edits, saved snapshots and changed saved plans are distinct;
  49    leaving a changed plan offers Save/Discard/Cancel.
  50  
  51  ## Catalogs, sites and rigs
  52  
  53  Targets support designation/common-name/alias search and custom entries.
  54  Coordinates are J2000; unknown size and magnitude stay absent.
  55  Rigs store camera/sensor/optics data, tracking, limits and provenance.
  56  Sites store coordinates, optional elevation/time zone and manual Bortle/SQM.
  57  GPS and a map can select a position; place-name lookup is optional.
  58  
  59  Metadata readers cover DNG, JPEG, HEIF still images and FITS.
  60  Recognition of a format does not imply a reader exists for it; unsupported
  61  formats and unreadable files return explicit outcomes. Metadata is reviewed
  62  before it creates or changes equipment.
  63  
  64  ## Results and data portability
  65  
  66  Users report completed-as-planned, partial frame counts, or not done with an
  67  optional reason; notes and observed conditions are optional.
  68  Session snapshots retain saved context for history. Legacy logs remain readable.
  69  There is no dedicated live-tracker screen.
  70  
  71  Logbook supports text sharing, portable session-file export and AstroBin
  72  acquisition CSV preview/sharing. AstroBin export is a file workflow.
  73  Settings provides local backup and confirmed restore through a staged restart.
  74  
  75  ## Offline and presentation
  76  
  77  Saved data, bundled catalogs and astronomy/planning calculations work offline.
  78  Forecast retrieval, map tiles and optional place-name lookup use the network.
  79  Weather retains source/fetch-time/cache information and missing-data states.
  80  Night calculations use UTC; local display respects the site's time context.
  81  
  82  Appearance supports System/Light/Dark plus a separate red field mode.
  83  Display units and weather presentation are configurable.
  84  The app plans and records; it does not acquire images or control hardware.

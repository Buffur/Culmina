  12  ## Current context adopters and states
  13  
  14  - **Tonight:** `lib/presentation/screens/tonight/tonight_home_screen.dart` (`_Context`) passes `compact:true`, `noSiteLabel:ContextLine.setSite` ("Set site"), the compact `SiteLabel` display and full `siteDescription:SiteLabel.of(siteVm)`. It leaves `framed:false` at its default, passes `planVm.eveningDate` and `zoneId`, and omits `nightStartUtc`, so no inline zone caption appears. `onSite/onNight` call `showHomeSiteSheet/showHomeNightSheet` in `home_context_sheets.dart`: Manage sites and the direct calendar respectively (TD-120).
  15  - **Planner:** `lib/presentation/screens/home/home_screen.dart` no longer constructs `ContextLine`. Its `HybridPlanRow` (`planner.context`, "Site and night") opens `PlanContextScreen` in `plan_context_screen.dart` via `openPlanContext`; site/night draft choices use Apply changes, while Close discards unapplied choices. The planner still uses `ContextLine.noSite` and `pickNight` for its copy action. Do not apply Tonight's compact/direct-calendar behavior to this flow.
  16  - **No site:** callers must supply `night:null` and no night instant; the component does not infer those from a null name. Tonight obtains the null date from `SessionPlanViewModel.eveningDate` / `sessionNight` when `SiteViewModel.isDefaultLocation` is true. The site action remains available as "Set site"; default component wording remains "No site set". Never compute a night for the placeholder location.
  17  - **Accessibility and layout:** each choice retains a 48 × 48 logical px minimum, an enabled button semantics action, its own Choose a site/night hint and a full-label tooltip. Compact choices follow their content with a 4 dp gap; the site flexes/ellipsizes when needed and the date keeps its natural width. Compact site semantics use `siteDescription ?? siteName ?? ContextLine.noSite`, while the default wrapping variant uses `siteName ?? ContextLine.noSite`. `noSiteLabel` changes visible wording, not the semantic fallback. Both variants expose the full "Night of …" label; `siteDescription` affects only compact site semantics/tooltip. `framed:true` remains an optional `context.card` wrapper for either variant, not a requirement for a screen.
  18  
  19  Callbacks and slots are the Flutter equivalents of editable properties and nested instances. Keep icon/text geometry stable when changing state, allow wrapping variants to wrap, retain compact variants' full-label semantics/tooltips and preserve separate accessible action names. Browser analogues do not establish actual routing or Material semantics.

  21  The reserved `design/` directory currently has no browser prototype. Use actual Flutter routes and tests; historical Visual Atlas evidence lives under `.audit/atlas/` and does not establish current router behavior.

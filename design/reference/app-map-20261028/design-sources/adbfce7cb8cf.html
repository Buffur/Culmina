   1  # Design system
   2  
   3  This is a reference to the implemented Flutter UI in `lib/`.
   4  It does not assert visual or accessibility acceptance.
   5  
   6  ## Design guidance structure
   7  
   8  Use the relevant one of the five local Culmina design skills. They have distinct
   9  scopes; supporting references and implementation files are not additional rule sets.
  10  Shared product invariants remain in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).
  11  
  12  | Local skill | Owns |
  13  | --- | --- |
  14  | [Components](../.agents/skills/culmina-design-components/SKILL.md) | Existing components, states, callbacks and actual adopters |
  15  | [Product rules](../.agents/skills/culmina-design-rules/SKILL.md) | Scientific meaning, time, missing data and persistence distinctions |
  16  | [Spacing](../.agents/skills/culmina-design-spacing/SKILL.md) | Local geometry, shape, density and reduced motion |
  17  | [Tokens](../.agents/skills/culmina-design-tokens/SKILL.md) | Semantic colors and surfaces across all three themes |
  18  | [Typography](../.agents/skills/culmina-design-typography/SKILL.md) | Adopted text roles, wrapping, scaling and numeric readability |
  19  
  20  This document indexes the implementation. The skills' supporting references route
  21  to current definitions and callers. Compatibility files in `.agents/rules/` point
  22  back to shared guidance; external design playbooks and archived recipes are not
  23  automatically adopted Culmina policy. A component specimen demonstrates appearance
  24  in its captured state, not production adoption or device acceptance.
  25  
  26  ## Theme sources
  27  
  28  - `core/theme/app_theme.dart`: Light, Dark and red Field `ThemeData`.
  29  - `app_colors.dart`: raw colors; `app_palette.dart`: semantic theme extension.
  30  - `hybrid_tokens.dart`: additional surfaces, selection, feedback, chart colors,
  31    compact type sizes, minimum target and app-bar height.
  32  - `app_typography.dart`, `app_spacing.dart`, `app_radius.dart`,
  33    `app_motion.dart`: type, spacing, shape and transition values.
  34  - `app_button_styles.dart`: shared button styling.
  35  
  36  `DisplaySettingsViewModel` stores System/Light/Dark appearance and display
  37  choices. `ThemeViewModel` stores field mode separately. `main.dart` selects
  38  the theme and applies `AppTheme.fieldFilter` around app content in field mode.
  39

  55  ## Typography
  56  
  57  Production text roles use the bundled **Geist** family.
  58  Source Serif 4 is bundled in `pubspec.yaml` but is not the production role family.
  59  
  60  | `AppTypography` role | Size / line height | Weight |
  61  | --- | --- | --- |
  62  | `screenTitle` | 22 / 27.5 | 500 |
  63  | `sectionHeading` | 20 / 28 | 600 |
  64  | `sectionSubject` | 18 / 23.4 | 500 |
  65  | `itemTitle` | 16 / 22.4 | 600 |
  66  | `rowTitle` | 16 / 24 | 500 |
  67  | `value` | 15 / 21.75 | 400 |
  68  | `body` | 14 / 21.7 | 400 |
  69  | `caption` | 13 / 19.5 | 400 |
  70  | `dense` | 12 / 18 | 400 |
  71  | `navigationLabel` | 11 / 16.5 | Caller selects 400/600 |
  72  
  73  Additional heading roles are `homeHeading` (29), `nightHeading` (25)
  74  and `answerHeading` (28). `AppTypography.scale` also defines the Material
  75  `TextTheme`; it is distinct from the named screen roles.
  76  Compact calendar/chart token sizes are 10/11 logical pixels.

  78  ## Layout, shape and motion
  79  
  80  - `AppSpacing`: 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48; page gutter 20 and section gap 28.
  81    Additional compact steps are 2, 3, 6, 10, 12 and 14.
  82  - `AppRadius`: small 6, menu 8, large 12 and pill 999.
  83  - `HybridTokens`: minimum target 48; app bar height 64.
  84  - `AppMotion`: short 120 ms, medium 180 ms, highlight 1500 ms;
  85    curve `Cubic(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
  86    Its duration helper returns zero when platform animations are disabled.
  87  - `AppShell`: four navigation items with minimum height 56;
  88    at text scale 1.5 or greater it lays them out two per row.
  89  
  90  ## Shared UI
  91  
  92  `presentation/shared/` contains the app bar, line icons, section headers,
  93  collapsible sections, detail scaffold, status/failure feedback, confirmation
  94  and deletion patterns, field-mode control and form-input helpers.
  95  `presentation/widgets/` contains altitude/night/weather visualizations,
  96  capture planning, budgets, fit/status and storage/gain presentation.
  97  Screen-specific layouts live under `presentation/screens/`.
  98  
  99  The current composition is Tonight → separate Plan/detail flows, with
 100  Logbook, Library and Settings as other root destinations.
 101  Labels, unknown-data messages and draft/result distinctions come from
 102  shared text helpers and the relevant screen/ViewModel state.

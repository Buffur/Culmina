1 # Design system
2
3 This is a reference to the implemented Flutter UI in `lib/`.
4 It does not assert visual or accessibility acceptance.
5
6 ## Design guidance structure
7
8 The five local Culmina design skills are the only project design authorities.
9 They cover all UI code, including screens, composed widgets, scientific displays,
10 dialogs, states and all themes; the gallery is only a scoped visual inventory.
11 Use the relevant skills during implementation. Supporting references and
12 implementation files are not additional rule sets. Extend the relevant Culmina
13 skill when guidance is missing. Technical and artifact helpers remain available
14 as described in [agent tooling](AGENT_TOOLING.md).
15 Shared product invariants remain in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).
16
17 | Local skill | Owns |
18 | --- | --- |
19 | [Components](../.agents/skills/culmina-design-components/SKILL.md) | Existing components, states, callbacks and actual adopters |
20 | [Product rules](../.agents/skills/culmina-design-rules/SKILL.md) | Scientific meaning, time, missing data and persistence distinctions |
21 | [Spacing](../.agents/skills/culmina-design-spacing/SKILL.md) | Local geometry, shape, density and reduced motion |
22 | [Tokens](../.agents/skills/culmina-design-tokens/SKILL.md) | Semantic colors and surfaces across all three themes |
23 | [Typography](../.agents/skills/culmina-design-typography/SKILL.md) | Adopted text roles, wrapping, scaling and numeric readability |
24
25 This document indexes the implementation. The skills' supporting references route
26 to current definitions and callers. Compatibility files in `.agents/rules/` point
27 back to shared guidance; external design playbooks and archived recipes are not
28 automatically adopted Culmina policy. A component specimen demonstrates appearance
29 in its captured state, not production adoption or device acceptance.

31 ## Theme sources
32
33 - `core/theme/app_theme.dart`: Light, Dark and red Field `ThemeData`.
34 - `app_colors.dart`: raw colors; `app_palette.dart`: semantic theme extension.
35 - `hybrid_tokens.dart`: additional surfaces, selection, feedback, chart colors,
36 compact type sizes, minimum target and app-bar height.
37 - `app_typography.dart`, `app_spacing.dart`, `app_radius.dart`,
38 `app_motion.dart`: type, spacing, shape and transition values.
39 - `app_button_styles.dart`: shared button styling.
40
41 `DisplaySettingsViewModel` stores System/Light/Dark appearance and display
42 choices. `ThemeViewModel` stores field mode separately. `main.dart` selects
43 the theme and applies `AppTheme.fieldFilter` around app content in field mode.
44

60 ## Typography
61
62 Production text roles use the bundled **Geist** family.
63 Source Serif 4 is bundled in `pubspec.yaml` but is not the production role family.
64

68 | `sectionHeading` | 20 / 28 | 600 |

78 Additional heading roles are `homeHeading` (29), `nightHeading` (25)
79 and `answerHeading` (28). `AppTypography.scale` also defines the Material
80 `TextTheme`; it is distinct from the named screen roles.
81 Compact calendar/chart token sizes are 10/11 logical pixels.

83 ## Layout, shape and motion
84
85 - `AppSpacing`: 4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48; page gutter 20 and section gap 28.
86 Additional compact steps are 2, 3, 6, 10, 12 and 14.
87 - `AppRadius`: small 6, menu 8, large 12 and pill 999.
88 - `HybridTokens`: minimum target 48; app bar height 64.
89 - `AppMotion`: short 120 ms, medium 180 ms, highlight 1500 ms;
90 curve `Cubic(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
91 Its duration helper returns zero when platform animations are disabled.
92 - `AppShell`: four navigation items with minimum height 56;
93 at text scale 1.5 or greater it lays them out two per row.

95 ## Shared UI
96
97 `presentation/shared/` contains the app bar, line icons, section headers,
98 collapsible sections, detail scaffold, status/failure feedback, confirmation
99 and deletion patterns, field-mode control and form-input helpers.
100 `presentation/widgets/` contains altitude/night/weather visualizations,
101 capture planning, budgets, fit/status and storage/gain presentation.
102 Screen-specific layouts live under `presentation/screens/`.
103
104 The current composition is Tonight → separate Plan/detail flows, with
105 Logbook, Library and Settings as other root destinations.
106 Labels, unknown-data messages and draft/result distinctions come from
107 shared text helpers and the relevant screen/ViewModel state.

17 `WeatherDetailsTable(rows:, background:)` in `widgets/weather_details_table.dart` implements the current Forecast details composition. It accepts the caller's nine existing cell widgets/row decorations and document surface color. One intrinsic `Table` fixes its leading Local time column at 96 dp; its actual render children keep painting, hit tests and semantic bounds synchronized during horizontal scroll. Row heights follow full text and the user's scaler without a second layout/measurement loop. Per-cell semantics retain reading order and expose metric cells beside time when accessibility requests reveal. Only the Weather details caller adopts it; loading/unavailable/no-dark branches, model/provenance, units, dated offsets/DST, unknowns and partial-hour qualifications remain caller-owned. `weather_fixed_time_test.dart` covers three themes/phone widths, scaling/direction/update smoke, reveal, exact metric/imperial DST readouts and snapshot integrity. The widget test does not establish physical-device, owner or TalkBack acceptance.

6 # Culmina typography
7
8 Use for text hierarchy, roles, weight, line height, wrapping and readable measurements. Read [AGENTS](../../../AGENTS.md), `lib/core/theme/app_typography.dart`, relevant compact roles in `hybrid_tokens.dart`, bundled assets in `pubspec.yaml` and affected text callers.
9
10 - Production text roles use Geist. Source Serif 4 remains bundled; its availability is not evidence of current role adoption. Preserve MaterialIcons and the separate line-icon rendering path.
11 - Read Material roles through `Theme.of(context).textTheme` and Culmina-specific roles through `AppTextTheme.of(context)`. `AppTypography` defines the central base scale; presentation does not read its static styles. Named roles and the Material TextTheme have distinct purposes.
12 - Keep font sizes, line heights, tracking and family declarations in the theme layer. Reuse the named role or a central `AppTextGeometry` preset through `AppTextTheme.resolve`; local `copyWith` may change semantic color, weight or numeric features, not geometry. Preserve existing dimensions when consolidating a role rather than silently normalizing screens.
13 - Diagnose available width, wrapping and padding before shrinking text. Preserve the user's text scaler and full accessible labels. Compact chart/calendar/navigation roles are explicit exceptions in current source, not a global size prescription.
14 - Check long names, errors, units and signs, ambiguous digits, and relevant Ukrainian glyphs (І і Ї ї Є є Ґ ґ). Use actual bundled fonts for visual evidence and tabular numerals where comparative measurements need them.
15 - Font-family selection applies only to a named family/design task. Compare identical content/composition, retain license/provenance and verify fallback; do not download or refresh assets as a side effect of a wrapping fix.
16
17 Report the role/caller, bounded correction or finding, checks and remaining limits. CSS specimens do not prove Flutter scaling, and static renders do not establish device or owner acceptance.
